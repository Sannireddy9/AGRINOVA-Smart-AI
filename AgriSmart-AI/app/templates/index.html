About AgriSmart AI for SIH 2026

AgriSmart AI is engineered to solve the Smart India Hackathon problem statement for smart agriculture. It combines transfer learning on deep convolutional backbones (ResNet-50) with completely dynamic class discovery. No disease categories are hardcoded, ensuring that when the SIH organizers provide the official class list at kickoff, the system adapts without code changes.
