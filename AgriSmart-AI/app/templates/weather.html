Agronomic Advisory Transparency & Threshold Notice

Rule-Based Heuristics (Not Machine Learning): This weather intelligence module operates strictly on deterministic agrometeorological rules. Configurable heuristic thresholds (e.g. rain probability ≥ 60%, heavy precipitation ≥ 10 mm, relative humidity ≥ 75%, heat ≥ 35°C, wind ≥ 20 km/h) are provided for demonstration and advisory purposes. Actual agronomic thresholds vary significantly by specific crop species, phenological growth stage, soil type, irrigation setup, and microclimate.

Zero-IoT Architecture: Soil moisture percentages and field parameters are entered manually by the farmer. This system does not require physical hardware sensors, ESP32 microcontrollers, or telemetry devices.

Data Provider & Refresh: Meteorological data is provided by Open-Meteo. Queries are cached for 15 minutes to respect rate limits. Clicking "Refresh Weather" triggers a single on-demand live update.
